# Notice

## Source

This dataset is compiled from official documents published by the Belgian
Chamber of Representatives (https://www.dekamer.be), including plenary and
committee reports, parliamentary documents and dossiers.

## What the license covers

The CC BY 4.0 license in this repository applies to the compilation: its
structure, cleaning, identifiers, linking and schema. No rights are claimed in
the underlying texts, which come from official Chamber documents. Their reuse
is governed by Belgian law, in particular art. XI.172 of the Code of Economic
Law, under which official acts are free of copyright and speeches in Chamber
sittings may be freely reproduced and communicated to the public. The Chamber
asks that its website be credited as the source.

## Not affiliated

This is an independent project. It is not published, endorsed or maintained by
the Belgian Chamber of Representatives.

## Personal data

Records name members of parliament, ministers and other speakers in their
public role, as published by the Chamber.

## Corrections and takedown

To report errors or request removal of a record, open an issue or email
info@kamerlens.be. Requests will be handled promptly.
